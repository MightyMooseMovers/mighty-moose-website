    Questions

    Good to know

    Is the price really free?

    Yes. The price tool is free, and you don't need to sign up or give us your email to use it.

    How do you set the final price?

    Send us a few photos or a short video of what's moving. We reply with one fixed price in writing. That's the price for the job we agreed on, even if moving day runs long.

    How does the deposit work?

    To hold your date, you pay 30% of the price. The rest is paid when the move is done. Cancel 72 hours or more before your move and you get your deposit back.

    How do I pay?

    E-transfer, card or cash. That goes for the deposit and for the rest of the price on moving day.

    Do you do junk removal too?

    Yes. We haul away furniture, appliances, mattresses and clutter, and we donate good furniture. The minimum price is $75. Get a junk removal price.

    Is there anything you don't move?

    We don't move pianos, safes or hot tubs, or anything dangerous like propane, gasoline or paint.

    What should I do before moving day?

    Pack and tape your boxes (or ask for packing help), empty your dresser drawers, and book the elevator if your building needs it. Our free moving checklist has the full list, week by week.
