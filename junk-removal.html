Questions

Good to know

What's the minimum price?

$75. Small single items start there. The price tool above shows the price for your job.

How do you set the price?

By how much space your junk takes in our trailer, how easy it is to reach, and how far away you are. Fridges, freezers and air conditioners have a $35 fee each because they need special handling. Send photos and we'll confirm one fixed price.

What can't you take?

Paint, propane tanks, chemicals and asbestos. The City of London's household special waste depot takes paint and chemicals.

Do you donate furniture?

Yes. If it's in good shape, it goes to a local charity instead of the landfill.

How do I pay?

E-transfer, card or cash. A 30% deposit holds your date, and the rest is paid when the job is done. Cancel 72 hours or more before and you get your deposit back.

Are you moving too?

We do both. Get a moving price, and add junk removal to the same visit.
